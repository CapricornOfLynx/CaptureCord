# Community code of conduct

Treat everyone with respect. Welcome different experience levels, backgrounds and viewpoints. Give specific, constructive feedback, focus on behavior and code, and accept corrections in good faith.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized attention, doxxing and publication of private information are unacceptable. Do not post credentials, personal endpoint identifiers or private communications without consent.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and temporarily or permanently restrict participation. Enforcement should be proportionate and explained when doing so is safe. Maintainers are subject to the same expectations.

Report concerns privately to an available repository maintainer through the repository hosting platform. A dedicated private reporting address must be established before a public community launch; do not publish sensitive incident details in public issues.
